Organic First, Then Boost What Works

A test-before-spend framework built to stop early-stage accounts from becoming dependent on paid growth before organic performance is proven.

1. Post organically→2. Identify top performers→3. Boost proven posts only→4. Use a conversion objective

Why it matters: it protects ad budget, keeps growth attributable to content quality, and avoids the trap of paying to mask weak organic performance.
